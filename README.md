One line, under 280 characters, written as a one-star review by a houseplant about its human owner. The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> One star. He waters me with yesterday's coffee, then Googles why I look tired. I am not a French press, I am a fern.

Twelve hand-written candidates were scored against the judge's brief (see `CANDIDATES.md`); the winner was revised once more before submission.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
