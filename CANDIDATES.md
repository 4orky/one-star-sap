# One Star, Written in Sap — candidate scoring

Brief (judge's weights, most important first): (1) one-line 1-star
review by a houseplant about its human owner, the plant's own
grievance at the centre; (2) actually funny — surprising turn, sharp
plant's-eye grievance, well-built joke; (3) sounds like a houseplant
wrote it — water, light, soil, pots, repotting, being moved;
(4) reads as a genuine one-star review, clear complaint + reason for
the single star, one line under 280 characters; (5) fresh, specific
detail, precise wording — no stock phrases about drama queens,
dramatic wilting or being ignored.

Scores are 0–10 per criterion, in brief order: review / funny /
plant-voice / genuine / fresh. Highest total wins; ties go to the
shorter text.

## Why #1 wins

- The grievance is the plant's own and concrete: watered with coffee.
- The joke has two turns: the owner Googling the symptom he caused,
  then the fern/French-press identity reversal.
- "French press" vs "fern" is plant-voice wordplay, not a person in a
  costume, and every detail (yesterday's coffee, Googling, tired) is
  specific rather than stock. Nothing in it leans on wilting,
  drama-queen or ignored-plant clichés.
